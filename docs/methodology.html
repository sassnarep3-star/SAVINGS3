2. Verification protocol — line by line

  1. Official domains only. Every number was fetched from the bank’s own website/document (e.g., marcus.com, chase.com rate-sheet PDFs) or a U.S. government site (fdic.gov, federalreserve.gov). No review sites, affiliates, forums, or AI summaries were used as evidence.
  2. Date-stamped. For each value we recorded the source page’s own “as of / effective” date. Verification sweep: October 22, 2026 (most rate pages are dated Oct 21, 2026; the exceptions are flagged, e.g., Truist Sep 28, 2026 — F-20).
  3. Blank beats guessed. If an official page would not render a number (ZIP gates, template tokens), the cell is blank, the account is excluded from rankings and return math, and the item is published on the Flagged for Review page with the official link so a human can check it with their ZIP.
  4. Conflicts are flagged, not averaged. Where official surfaces disagreed (search snippets, affiliate URLs, stale caches), we published the canonical page value and logged the conflict (F-05, F-06, F-09).
  5. Full audit trail. Every claim on this site traces to a line in research-notes.md with its URL and source date. Work was coordinated via COORDINATION.md in the repository.

3. Badges

BadgeMeaning
VERIFIEDThe number (and its as-of date) was read directly from the official page on Oct 22, 2026, with no extra conditions for a standard new account.
CONDITIONALVerbatim from an official page, but the headline APY needs something: a minimum/maintained balance, a linked product, new-customer status, an eligible ZIP/state, or it comes from a market-specific rate sheet. The condition is spelled out in the row — never ranked as if unconditional.
UNVERIFIEDCould not confirm the value from an official source. Cell left blank; excluded from rankings and ROI; see Flagged for Review.
BENCHMARKA government statistic (e.g., FDIC national average), not an account you can open.

4. Ranking rules

  • Accounts are ranked by verified unconditional APY first. Conditional APYs are shown in the same tables but labeled, with the qualifying conditions and the fallback rate where verified.
  • “Up to” headline rates are never presented as the typical rate; the tier that produces the headline is stated ($25k+, $250k+, etc.).
  • Bonuses are compared with simple % = bonus ÷ required balance, always next to the holding period, and never called an APY. Direct-deposit bonuses get no % (there is no balance to divide by).
  • Competitiveness is measured against the FDIC national averages of Oct 19, 2026 (savings 0.38%, money market 0.63%, 12-mo CD 1.76%, 60-mo CD 1.41%): multiple = APY ÷ average.
  • Fees matter: where a monthly fee can erase interest, the break-even balance is computed and shown (Traditional Savings page).

5. Return math

  • Growth: FV = P × (1 + APY)t; Interest = FV − P. Deposit sizes P = $10,000 / $50,000 / $100,000 / $250,000; horizons t = 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 years from the open date October 22, 2026.
  • Assumptions (always printed with the tables): APY unchanged for the whole horizon, no additional deposits/withdrawals, interest left to compound, before fees and taxes. For variable APYs this is a hypothetical illustration, not a forecast. For CDs held to maturity the APY is contractually fixed, so the same formula gives the maturity figure (sub-year CD amounts are APY-formula estimates; a bank’s day-count can differ by cents — cross-checked against Capital One’s own earnings example within rounding).
  • FDIC-safe maximum initial deposit: Pmax = $250,000 ÷ (1 + APY)t (single ownership category, no other deposits at that bank), because the $250,000 limit covers principal plus accrued interest.
  • Only verified/conditional APYs enter the math. Unverified APYs produce no return figures anywhere on the site.
  • Figures were computed programmatically and rounded to cents; recompute any cell with the calculator on the Returns page.

6. Limitations & freshness

  • Rates are a snapshot. Variable APYs can change daily; several banks reprice by market/ZIP. Anything you act on should be re-confirmed on the official page (linked in every row) on the day you open.
  • Text extraction risk. Some bank pages render rates via scripts/tokens that text fetches can’t see — that is exactly why those values are blank rather than copied from third parties (Flagged page).
  • No personalized advice. Taxes, state law, estate/beneficiary design and your liquidity needs are out of scope. For coverage above $250,000 use the FDIC’s EDIE estimator or a professional adviser.
  • Corrections welcome. Flagged items include “what to check manually” instructions; repository issues/PRs can update docs/research-notes.md and the affected table.
