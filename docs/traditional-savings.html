Traditional (branch) savings accounts

These are the standard savings accounts sold in branches. Verified honestly: they all pay 0.01%–0.04% APY — below the FDIC national average of 0.38% (Oct 19, 2026) — and several charge a monthly fee that can cost more than the interest earns (see the fee table). Their value is branch access and bundled services, not yield. If earning interest matters, compare the High-Yield category.
