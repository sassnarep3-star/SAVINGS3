Opening rules & eligibility

Under federal Customer Identification Program rules, every U.S. bank must verify your identity with your legal name, date of birth, residential address, and an identification number (SSN/ITIN) — expect that everywhere below, plus identity-verification questions and approval checks. Digital app & access

A digital app was a requirement for this comparison, so every charter below is openable/manageable digitally (the online-first banks — Marcus, Ally, Amex, Capital One, Citizens Access — have no branch network at all, while Chase, BofA, Wells, Citi, U.S. Bank, PNC, Truist, TD and Citizens add branches/ATMs). Practical rules:

  • Download the app only from the link on the bank’s official website (linked throughout this site) or by searching the exact publisher name in the Apple App Store / Google Play. Never install a banking app from a text-message or email link.
  • Online-only HYSAs move money by ACH transfer from a linked external bank — transfers typically take 1–3 business days and several banks cap outgoing transfers for new accounts (Amex: $1,000 in the first 30 days). Keep a checking account elsewhere for instant access; don’t park next week’s rent in a brand-new HYSA.
  • Type the bank’s domain yourself or use this site’s official-source links; check the domain matches exactly (e.g., marcus.com, capitalone.com) before entering credentials.

Security & verifying FDIC insurance yourself

Don’t take this site’s word for FDIC coverage — verify in 60 seconds:

  1. Open the FDIC’s BankFind Suite and search the charter name from the table below (e.g., “Goldman Sachs Bank USA”, not “Marcus”). The certificate page confirms the bank is FDIC-insured and shows its official name — the name that must be on your account disclosures.
  2. Enter your balances in the FDIC’s EDIE (Electronic Deposit Insurance Estimator) to confirm coverage across ownership categories, especially above $250,000 or with joint/trust accounts.
  3. Re-read the FDIC FAQ: $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category, principal plus interest.

Account-security checklist (general guidance)

  • Turn on multi-factor authentication and balance/transfer alerts in the app; use a unique password (password manager) and device biometrics.
  • A bank will never ask you by phone/text/email to move money “to a safe account” or to read out a one-time code. Hang up and call the number on the bank’s official site or your card.
  • Interest is taxable: expect a 1099-INT. Bonuses are generally reported as interest too — each offer page states its treatment.
  • Re-verify rates before funding large deposits: rates on this site were true on Oct 21–22, 2026 and will drift. For $250k-scale deposits, use the FDIC-safe planner first.
