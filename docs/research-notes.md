## Math (reproducible)

Variable-APY projections: FV = P×(1+APY)^t; Interest = FV−P; t = 0.5/1/2/3/5; P = $10k/$50k/$100k/$250k; assumes APY unchanged, no deposits/withdrawals/fees/taxes, interest stays in account. **Variable APYs will change — projections are hypothetical illustrations, not promises.** CDs: same formula to maturity only (fixed APY if held to maturity; early-withdrawal penalties excluded). Sub-year CD figures are APY-formula estimates; bank day-count may differ slightly (Capital One's own earnings table cross-checks within $1 rounding on $5k basis). FDIC-safe max initial deposit for horizon t (single category, no other deposits at bank): Pmax = $250,000/(1+APY)^t. Bonus % = bonus ÷ required balance (simple, before interest/fees/taxes; holding period shown — not an APY).
