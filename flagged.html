Patterns worth knowing about

  • The biggest banks hide rates behind a ZIP code. Chase, Citi, TD and Bank of America all render rates client-side or per location. That is a marketing choice, not a data gap on this side — and it is why those banks' APY cells are blank while their fees and rules are fully transcribed.
  • Marketing pages contain template and arithmetic defects. Bank of America printed "$XXXX" as a fee, Capital One printed "NaN APY", and Synchrony printed both 3.55% and 2.85% as its own APY. None of those defects were smoothed over.
  • Second-hand numbers on first-party pages are still second-hand. Bank comparison widgets quote competitor rates sourced from Curinos. They are not treated as verification for anything here.
