Inclusion rules

  • Large national banks first. The list covers the largest branch networks (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citibank, U.S. Bank, PNC, Truist, TD, Fifth Third, Citizens) plus national digital banks that are themselves FDIC-insured and app-first (Capital One, Ally, American Express, Marcus/Goldman Sachs, Synchrony, SoFi, Schwab). Small local banks and credit unions are out of scope by request.
  • Every bank must be an FDIC-insured institution. Confirmed through FDIC institution records, not the bank's marketing.
  • A rate row needs a number from the bank. If the bank would not print it, the row keeps its fees and rules but the APY is blank.
  • No aggregators. NerdWallet, Bankrate, Forbes, depositaccounts and similar were not used as sources for any value on this site, even where they agree with the numbers shown.

Arithmetic

Interestending balance − deposit
Annual interest (displayed)deposit × APY / 100
Tier selectionThe highest tier whose minimum balance is at or below the deposit (tiered example)
BenchmarkFDIC national savings rate 0.38%, effective 2026-10-19 (FDIC source)
Annualised bonus equivalentbonus ÷ deposit × 365 ÷ required holding days (example offer)

An APY already reflects compounding, so (1 + APY) is the correct one-year factor for a lump sum. Holding the same APY for N years is a model: every account on this site is variable-rate except the CDs in the context table.

Limits of this research

  • Rates were read from public pages, not from inside an authenticated account. A logged-in customer may see a different, personalised rate — U.S. Bank and TD both say so explicitly.
  • Rate pages that require a ZIP code or client-side rendering may show different values for a New York address than the ones captured. Those cases are flagged.
  • APYs are variable unless labelled fixed. Nothing here forecasts where rates go.
  • Tax is not modelled. Interest is taxable income in the year it is credited.
  • Account-opening identity requirements, funding windows and bonus eligibility were transcribed where the bank printed them; they were not tested by opening an account.

Version history

This dashboard is the second research pass and replaces a shorter earlier snapshot, which is kept unchanged at site/index.html. Both passes agree on every overlapping rate; where they differ in scope, this pass is the superset. The reconciliation is logged in COORDINATION.md.

Reproducing the site

The pages are generated, not hand-edited: python3 scripts/build_site.py reads data/*.json and rewrites the HTML. Every fact lives in the JSON with its source URL, so a change to a rate is one edit and one rebuild.
