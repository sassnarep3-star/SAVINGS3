How to read the APY column

  • A number means the bank's own page printed that percentage, and the "as of" date is what the bank printed next to it. If the bank printed no date, the cell says so.
  • blank means no official page in this session displayed a rate. Chase, Citi and TD all gate their rate tables behind a ZIP code or client-side script. Third-party rate trackers do publish numbers for those banks; none of them are used here.
  • Tiered accounts (U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo Platinum) show the standard rate; the tiers are spelled out in the row and used in the returns tables.
  • Variable means the bank can change the APY at any time, including right after the account is opened. Only CDs are fixed.
