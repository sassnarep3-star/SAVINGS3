Rules that void or reduce a bonus

  • Chase: not available to existing Chase savings customers, or accounts closed in the last 90 days, or closed with a negative balance in the last 3 years. Money must be new to Chase. Bonus determined on day 30; balance must be held 90 days from enrollment. Offer expires 01/27/2027.
  • Wells Fargo: the 3.25% APY is for new savings customers only and requires $25,000+ deposited and maintained within 30 days. Below $25,000 the standard Platinum Savings rate is 0.01%. The $12 monthly fee is waived only with a $3,500 daily balance.
  • SoFi: the +1.10% boost needs an eligible direct deposit set up within 60 days and maintained, and lasts up to six months. Opening a SoFi Savings account also opens a SoFi Checking account.
  • Marcus: the +1.00% runs three months; after that the standard APY applies.
  • Fees can eat a bonus. A $5 monthly fee on a $1,000 balance is 0.50% a year — larger than most of the APY differences on this site.

Banks with no promotion found on the official page

Bank of America, Citi, U.S. Bank, Truist, TD, Schwab, Ally, American Express and Capital One had no savings sign-up bonus or APY promotion on the pages captured. Capital One, American Express and Wells Fargo do publish CD specials; those are fixed-rate products, listed on the rate check page. Absence of a promotion on one captured page is not proof none exists — promotional offers are frequently targeted.
