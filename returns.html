Return on Deposit

Interest and ending balances for deposits from $10,000 to $1,250,000 at every verified APY, for 1, 3 and 5 years.

What a deposit actually earns at the size you are planning. All figures are arithmetic on the APY the bank printed — no projections, no assumptions about future rate moves. A 2026-10-23 opening is assumed; year 1 ends 2026-10-23 of the following year.

Formula used: balance = deposit × (1 + APY)years, interest = balance − deposit. An advertised APY already reflects compounding, so this reproduces a bank's own annual disclosure while holding the rate constant. Variable-rate accounts will not hold the rate for 3 or 5 years; those columns show what the same rate would produce, not a forecast.
