Coverage is not fraud insurance

FDIC deposit insurance addresses insured bank failure, not a promise to reimburse every scam or unauthorized transaction. Safeguards and reimbursement policies are distinct; read applicable bank terms. Never share one-time codes; protect the linked email and phone as well as the bank login. FDIC coverage ↗
