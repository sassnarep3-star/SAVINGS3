A simpler buffer strategy

Using a $240,000 opening deposit at Marcus produces a modeled $8,400 interest and $248,400 balance after one year, leaving $1,600 of unused coverage if the 3.50% APY holds. For larger savings, split funds among separately insured banks rather than merely opening more accounts or using more branches of the same bank.

Formula & rate risk

Interest = principal × ((1 + APY/100)^(days/365) − 1)
Opening ceiling = floor(250000 / (1 + APY/100) × 100) / 100

For $100,000, a full-year APY of 2.50%, 3.50%, or 4.50% would yield $2,500, $3,500, or $4,500. These are hypothetical sensitivity cases, not forecasts. The savings account has no guaranteed investment lifespan; the 92/182/365-day horizons are planning windows. CDs may lock rates but bring term and withdrawal constraints; no ambiguous CD rate has been attributed to a specific term here.
