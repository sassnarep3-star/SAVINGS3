Method

Reviewed the starting repository line by line: it contained only a one-line README and no financial dataset to preserve. Selected nationally marketed banks with major consumer brands; avoided small local institutions and fintech pass-through accounts. Official retrieval took priority over search snippets. Conflicting or unconfirmed numeric values are empty and flagged. Third-party comparison-bank rates displayed on Marcus are not used as verification of competitors. Bank marketing multiples of the national average are not adopted as independent benchmarks.

Every table row has official source links. Computed outputs are labeled and use only confirmed base APYs. Full terms, personal eligibility, and real-time underwriting cannot be confirmed by public-page research alone.
