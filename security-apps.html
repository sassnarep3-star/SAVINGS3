The security questions that actually matter for a large insured balance

  • Deposit insurance is the real backstop. A bank's app security protects your access; the FDIC protects the balance if the bank fails. Both matter, and they are not substitutes.
  • Check the certificate, not the logo. Every bank here is checked against FDIC institution records; two brands can share one certificate, and one inactive record (Discover Bank) is excluded from the returns tables.
  • Turn on the strongest login the bank offers. Where a bank publishes a security guarantee (Ally publishes an online and mobile security guarantee), read its conditions before relying on reimbursement.
  • Prefer push or app-based approval over SMS. This is general guidance, not a claim about any specific bank on this page.
  • Verify transfers out. Every account here allows external transfers. A large balance in a single-bank savings account is reachable by anyone who gets account access.
